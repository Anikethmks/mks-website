MKS Vision delivers innovation that meets industrial practicality, transforming ideas into tangible solutions to reduce product design lead time, meeting cost & quality requirements and accelerate time to market.

Our capabilities

Engineering services organized the way an industrial OEM works.

Design and development

  • New product development — concept to release-ready design
  • Design automation — rules-based configurators
  • Engineering Data migration — legacy 2D/3D data to modern platforms, Detailing, GD&T
  • Reverse engineering — scan, measure, rebuild & improve
  • Engineering analysis — FEA, CFD and tolerance stack-ups
  • Reliability — System level qualification through 10 step DFR Methodology
  • ECR / ECO — change requests through release

Manufacturing and Quality

  • Manufacturing — process plans, Jigs and fixtures, Welding and special process qualification
  • PPAP — complete submission packages
  • SPC — control charts and Process capability studies
  • CMM — inspection programming and reports
  • Failure Analysis — 8D/7 QC tools

Supply Chain Management

  • Cost out analysis — Teardown and Should-cost models
  • VAVE — value engineering on existing products
  • Sourcing — Ownership from supplier identification till Part/Assembly qualification until shipment
  • Aftermarket support — Technical manuals, obsolescence, Product sustenance and Warranty analysis
  • SQE — Supplier management, product quality and compliance management

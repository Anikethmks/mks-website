Overview

Transforming a Critical Quality Control Process

From manual, error-prone certification to a real-time automated pipeline

A Global Precision Manufacturing Leader in the Industrial and Heavy Equipment sector manages an intricate supply chain that demands rigorous quality validation of incoming raw materials. Central to this is the Supplier Material Certification process, a high-frequency, detail-intensive workflow where supplier-submitted chemical composition certificates must be extracted, validated, and recorded against ERP quality specifications before raw material lots can be approved or rejected.

MKS Vision partnered with this manufacturer to design and deploy a fully automated UiPath RPA solution that eliminated manual intervention across the entire certification lifecycle — from email retrieval through ERP submission and folder management — transforming a process prone to delays and human error into a near real-time, highly accurate automated pipeline.

The Challenge

Manual Certification Was Slowing Down the Supply Chain

High costs, inconsistent processes, and manual workflows inhibited growth and quality

High Time Cost

Each certificate took 1–20 minutes to process manually, creating significant throughput bottlenecks during peak periods and delaying raw material lot approvals.

Human Error Risk

Manual data extraction from varied certificate formats led to a ~10% exception rate from incomplete details, formatting issues, and validation mismatches.

Resource Drain

Skilled supply chain staff were tied up in repetitive, low-value data entry instead of exception management, supplier engagement, and strategic procurement work.

Scalability Constraints

Volume spikes required additional headcount to maintain SLAs, making the process costly to scale without proportional and unsustainable staffing increases.

The Supplier Material Certification process ran entirely on manual effort. Supply chain staff were required to retrieve supplier certificates from email, extract chemical composition values by hand, cross-reference each value against ERP quality specifications, and manually log results — repeating this cycle every 30 minutes, processing up to 100 certificates per day.

The Objective

Build a Touchless, End-to-End Certification Pipeline

A connected, agile, and fully automated certification process ready for the future

✓
  • Automate retrieval of supplier certificate files from designated Outlook email folders
  • Dynamically fetch quality Test IDs from the ERP via API to guide targeted data extraction
  • Extract all required chemical composition values, mechanical properties, and order metadata from certificate PDFs
  • Submit structured validation data to the ERP per quantity lot, receiving pass/fail determinations in real time
  • Route processed certificates to Success or Failure FTP folders automatically based on API response codes
  • Implement robust exception handling with automated notifications for unresolvable errors and failures

MKS Vision was engaged to fully automate the Supplier Material Certification workflow using UiPath RPA, eliminating manual touchpoints while maintaining the accuracy and compliance standards required for raw material approval in a regulated manufacturing environment.

The Impact

Measurable Outcomes Across Speed, Accuracy, and Cost

A supply chain ready for the next generation of precision manufacturing excellence

The deployment of the UiPath RPA solution delivered immediate, quantifiable benefits across every dimension of the Supplier Material Certification process. The transformation was comprehensive, measurable, and sustainable:

95% Faster Processing

Certificate processing time dropped from up to 20 minutes per item to under 1 minute, enabling near real-time lot approval and preventing costly supply chain delays.

99.5% Accuracy Rate

Automated extraction and structured API validation virtually eliminated the human error rate, reducing exceptions from ~10% to near zero and ensuring audit-ready data quality.

4 FTEs Redeployed

Supply chain staff previously dedicated to manual certification tasks were freed to focus on exception management, supplier relationships, and strategic procurement activities.

$340,000 Annual Savings

Based on fully-loaded US FTE costs, the automation delivers approximately $340,000 in annual labor savings with full ROI realized within the first year of deployment.

Beyond the numbers, the solution established a scalable, resilient automation foundation. The bot handles 10–100 certificates daily without degradation, operates on a continuous 30-minute cycle, and is architected to accommodate future certificate formats or ERP schema changes with minimal rework.

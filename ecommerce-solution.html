Built to standard

Industry standards we design and test against

Every store we ship is measured against the benchmarks that shoppers, search engines, payment networks, and regulators already use — not just a feature checklist.

PCI DSS v4.0.1

Secure payments

Hosted or tokenized checkouts that keep card data off your servers and shrink your compliance scope, plus payment-page script monitoring and integrity controls.

WCAG 2.2 AA

Accessible by design

Keyboard-friendly navigation, readable contrast, and screen-reader support, so more people can buy from you and you're ready for accessibility laws like the European Accessibility Act.

Core Web Vitals

Fast on every device

We build to Google's "good" thresholds: LCP ≤ 2.5s, INP ≤ 200ms, and CLS ≤ 0.1. Image optimisation, caching, and a CDN are part of every build.

OWASP Top 10

Secure engineering

Secure coding practices, dependency scanning, MFA on admin access, and regular patching, so storefronts and admin panels stay protected.

GDPR · CCPA

Privacy & consent

Consent management, data minimisation, and customer data-request workflows that respect privacy rules in the markets where you sell.

MACH principles

Composable architecture

Microservices, API-first, cloud-native, and headless, so you can replace or scale any part of the stack without rebuilding the whole store.

Compliance certifications such as PCI DSS are assessed for each merchant. We build and document your platform so those assessments are as simple as possible.

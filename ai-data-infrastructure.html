Every megawatt that powers AI must be delivered safely — and removed as heat

Every AI response starts with “electricity” from the grid, flowing through substations, switchgear, transformers, generators, UPS and rack power distribution units to reach the server, and ends as “heat” from racks drawing many times the power of a traditional server room. Today’s AI data centres work as one system, made up of power plants, cooling plants and digital networks.

MKS Vision is an engineering services partner to manufacturers building power generation, infrastructure and Cooling Management systems, bringing hands-on engineering experience across multiple domains and working as their extended engineering team.

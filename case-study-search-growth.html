The challenge

Most of the catalogue was invisible online

A June 2026 audit found that most products could not be seen by Google, shopping channels or AI assistants, and ad reporting mixed B2B and B2C sales together. The site scored 3.1 out of 10 overall.

Google AdsB2B orders counted as B2C conversions, so bidding and ROAS reporting could not separate the two
What we did

Four workstreams, one product-data foundation

We fixed the product data once, then used it across ads, search, AI assistants and marketplaces.

016-week programme

Google Ads

  • Audited the account and removed unwanted conversion actions that inflated results
  • Set up server-side tracking so purchases are recorded reliably, even when browsers block cookies
  • Tagged every order as B2C or B2B and fed it back to Google with the click ID
  • Restructured campaigns around a Performance Max campaign for best-selling products
  • Built a reporting hub showing spend, realised ROAS, profit on ad spend (POAS) and the B2B/B2C split
0216-week programme, live from August 2026

AI SEO

  • Wrote SEO titles and meta descriptions, and cleaned up URLs
  • Added structured data: Product, review ratings, Organization, LocalBusiness for each shop, FAQ and breadcrumbs
  • Improved site speed by reducing JavaScript and CSS, optimising fonts and using a CDN
  • Delivered 42 activities in weeks 1 to 6; 40 live and 2 ongoing
03Running alongside AI SEO

Agentic Commerce readiness

  • Made product data machine-readable so AI shopping assistants can understand and recommend products
  • Added FAQ content and schema, the main trigger for inclusion in Google AI Overviews
  • Prepared the catalogue for Google’s Shopping Graph and for in-assistant checkout where Shopify supports it
04Launch phase

Amazon Marketplace

  • Planned an Amazon Seller Central (Ireland/UK) launch synced with Shopify, so stock and prices stay in one place
  • Checked barcode (GTIN) data across the catalogue, a requirement for listing
  • Selected best-selling products for the first listings, starting with merchant-fulfilled orders and Sponsored Products ads

What’s next

Scaling what works, opening new channels

AI SEO

Finish the 16-week programme in November 2026, and publish the remaining online-ready products with full content.

Google Ads

Move to target-ROAS bidding, add a dedicated B2B campaign and test YouTube ads, all reported through the new reporting hub.

Agentic Commerce

Track how often AI Overviews, ChatGPT and Perplexity cite the store for key purchase queries.
